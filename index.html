Lifestyle AI (“Lifestyle AI”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a general wellness and lifestyle app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Lifestyle AI mobile application.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

  • Authentication information provided when you sign in, including your Firebase Authentication identifier.
  • Your email address, display name, and profile image when supplied by your selected sign-in provider.

1.2 Information you enter in the app

  • Profile information, including information such as age, height, weight, activity level, goals, food preferences, and lifestyle preferences.
  • Food logs, meal descriptions, exercise logs, plans, grocery items, and notes you choose to save.
  • Messages and prompts you submit to Lifestyle AI Coach.
  • Photos, text, or audio that you intentionally submit for supported app features, such as food or meal analysis.

1.3 Technical and diagnostic information

  • Device type, operating system version, app version, and technical error information used to diagnose crashes and improve app reliability.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Lifestyle AI.
  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Store and synchronize your saved app data across your devices.
  • Provide AI-assisted features when you request them.
  • Process and restore subscription access.
  • Diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve app reliability.
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests.

3. Service providers

We use service providers to operate Lifestyle AI. Depending on the features you use, these may include:

  • Firebase: authentication, cloud database storage, and crash reporting.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement and purchase management.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: payment processing, subscription management, and purchase restoration.
  • OpenAI: AI-assisted text, image, and audio processing when you use an AI feature.

When you submit text, images, or audio to an AI feature, the content needed to process your request may be sent to OpenAI. We use this information only to provide the requested AI feature.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and service terms.

4. Data storage and security

Account-linked app data may be stored using Firebase services. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect your information. No internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. How we share information

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the app, provide requested features, process purchases, and improve reliability.
  • We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid government request.

6. Your choices

  • You can manage device permissions, such as camera, microphone, and photo access, in your device settings.
  • You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings and Google Play subscriptions through your Google Play account settings.
  • You can request account and associated-data deletion using the in-app Delete Account option when available, or by contacting us using the email address below.

7. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Lifestyle AI account and associated account-linked data from within the app when the Delete Account feature is available.

Deleting a Lifestyle AI account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. You must separately manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account or Google Play account settings.

If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by emailing lifestyleai26@gmail.com with the subject line Lifestyle AI – Account Deletion Request.

8. Children’s privacy

Lifestyle AI is not directed to children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. General wellness only

Lifestyle AI is intended for general wellness, lifestyle, educational, and informational purposes only. It does not provide medical, clinical, diagnostic, or emergency advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical or dietary decisions.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

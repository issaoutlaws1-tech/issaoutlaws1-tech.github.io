Lifestyle AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Lifestyle AI (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a general wellness and productivity app designed to help users log food and exercise, view simple balance information, and receive AI-generated suggestions. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

By using Lifestyle AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

  • Email address and basic profile information if you sign in with Google or another identity provider.
  • Authentication identifiers provided by Firebase Authentication.

1.2 App usage and content

  • Food logs, meal descriptions, exercise logs, and related notes you enter.
  • Photos you choose to upload (for example, food photos, nutrition labels, or kitchen scale images).
  • Basic app usage information such as screens visited, features used, and timestamps.

1.3 Device and technical data

  • Device type, operating system, app version.
  • Anonymous identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide core app functionality (logging, history, balance calculations).
  • Store your data and sync it across devices (via Firebase and related cloud services).
  • Generate AI-assisted suggestions and analyses (for example, meal ideas or nutrition insights).
  • Improve the app through analytics and crash reports.
  • Communicate with you about important updates or changes.

3. AI and third-party services

Lifestyle AI uses third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Firebase (for authentication, database, storage, analytics, and/or crash reporting).
  • OpenAI and similar AI providers (for text, vision, and audio processing).
  • Optional web search providers (when enabled) to retrieve additional information.

When you submit text, images, or audio for AI features, this content may be sent to these third-party providers to generate responses or analyses. We only send the minimum data necessary to provide the requested functionality.

These providers process your data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Data storage and security

Your data may be stored on secure servers managed by Firebase or other cloud providers. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We may share data with service providers (such as Firebase, AI providers, and analytics tools) strictly to operate and improve the app.
  • We may share information with authorities if required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Aggregated or anonymized information that does not identify you personally may be used for analytics, research, or improving the service.

6. Your choices and controls

  • You may choose to use certain features without signing in; in that case, data may be stored locally on your device or in an anonymous form.
  • If you sign in, you may request deletion of your account-linked data by contacting us (see Account Deletion below).
  • You can manage permissions (camera, microphone, photos, etc.) through your device settings.

7. Children’s privacy

Lifestyle AI is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. General wellness, not medical advice

Lifestyle AI is intended for general wellness and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical, nutritional, or clinical advice. You should always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical or dietary decisions.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union, where our service providers operate. By using the app, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page when changes are made. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
